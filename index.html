                Backup and recovery

                Privacy

                • Stored on this device only: your notes, facts, tasks, reminders, summaries, conversation history, settings and the Backend URL. They live in this browser's local storage for this website. Clearing your browser data, or using a private tab, removes them.
                • Backup: use Download backup above and keep the file somewhere safe. The app has no cloud sync.
                • Offline mode: nothing leaves your device, except optional weather lookups (city name sent to Open-Meteo) and browser voice recognition.
                • Online AI mode: each message you send, plus any recent messages and items you ticked under Memory, goes to your backend and then to the AI provider you configured. Your whole memory is never sent automatically.
                • Provider retention: the AI provider may keep or log requests under its own policy. Deleting your history here does not delete anything held by the provider; check its privacy settings.
                • Not stored: passwords, tokens, API keys and card numbers are refused.
